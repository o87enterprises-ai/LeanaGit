I'm a proud Oakland native, mama bear, and community leader running for the Oakland School Board to fight for District 6's cubs — every single one of them.

Born and raised in Oakland, my family has been in our town's public schools for five generations. I have two cubs currently in Oakland Unified School District and one who graduated from high school. Oakland is home, and the success of the district is the success of my children, my extended family, my neighbors, and my community. For this mama bear, it doesn't get more personal than that.

As a mother of three — some experiencing disabilities — I know firsthand the challenges families face navigating public education while advocating for their cubs' success. One of my children graduated high school during the unprecedented pandemic, giving me direct experience with the academic, emotional, and social impacts that families still carry today.

I am active in the Parent Teacher Organization (PTO) at Burckhalter Elementary, where deep collaboration between families, educators, school staff, and administration has shown me something important: advocacy and collaboration are not opposites. A real mama bear knows when to roar and when to build bridges — effective leadership requires both.

My professional experience includes budget and policy advocacy locally and at the California State Capitol; supporting and building capacity for education nonprofits that provide much-needed resources to children and families; leading parent workshops on special needs and behavioral health; engaging public officials to bring more resources to our community; and creating spaces to empower parents and strengthen community voices. When you put me on the board, you're putting a mama bear in the room — one who won't stop fighting until every cub in District 6 gets the education they deserve.
