It is unacceptable that less than 25% of students in District 6 are reading and doing math on grade level. If our students are not fluent readers by third grade, they will not be able to be successful in other subjects in school. Rather than setting them up for success in college, career and community, we are funneling our children into the school-to-prison pipeline.

Last year, the board’s only direction to the Superintendent as she prepared the budget was “no school closures”—that resulted in deep and painful cuts at every school site, including urgently needed Literacy Coaches and Early Reading Tutors, and the decimation of the Office of Equity at the central level. These are exactly the kinds of support that our students need if they are to break out of the current cycle. If we don’t have a vision for our district that is centered on student outcomes, then even the most dedicated educators will not be able to help our students succeed.
